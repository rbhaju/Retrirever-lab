L'Étude de Conservation · Privacy Notice.

Version 1.0 · revised June 11, 2026

Atelier Rohan is composed upon a single principle: that the record of what you own is yours alone. This notice sets out, plainly, how your information is treated. It is written for clarity, and is not a substitute for legal counsel.

Your cabinet stays on your device — and the studio is built so that it cannot be otherwise.

The cabinet

Every formulation you record — its brand, its ingredients, the day you unsealed it, its place in your routine — is stored locally upon your device. Retriever Labs operates no server for this purpose and receives no copy. We do not know what you collect.

iCloud synchronisation

Should you remain signed into iCloud, your cabinet is synchronised to your own private iCloud account and shared between the Apple devices that share that account. This passage moves directly between your device and Apple. The studio neither sees nor stores it.

The optical scanner

When you read a label or a period-after-opening marker, the image is processed entirely upon your device using Apple's Vision framework. Neither the photograph nor the text drawn from it is ever transmitted over the network. The original image, if retained, is kept locally beside the record it describes.

The Maison Registry

When you search for a brand, an ingredient list, or a barcode, the application may query Open Beauty Facts, an open and public registry, to autofill those details at your request. Such a query carries only the term you are searching for. It does not carry your identity, your cabinet, or any record of what you own.

No account

There is no account to create, no password to keep, and no electronic mail address required to use Atelier Rohan. You are not registered with us, because there is no register.

No advertisement, no measurement

The application contains no advertising, no advertising identifier, no analytics, no crash telemetry, and no tracking of any kind. Such cookies as the website employs are functional only, and serve no measurement.

Drawing out your records

Your records may be exported at any time and taken wherever you wish. Because the cabinet resides on your device, removing the application removes the data with it; where iCloud is in use, you may also remove the cabinet from your iCloud account through Apple's settings.

Children

Atelier Rohan is intended for adult collectors and is not directed to children. We knowingly gather nothing from anyone, of any age.

Revisions

Should this notice be revised, the version and date above will be amended accordingly. Material changes will be reflected within the application.

Correspondence

Questions regarding this notice are most welcome. You may write to us by way of the Correspondence page.
