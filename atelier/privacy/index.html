Privacy Notice.

Version 1.0 · revised June 13, 2026

The atelier is built for one. Your cabinet, your inventory, your collection — kept in private custody. Below is a complete accounting of how Atelier Rohan treats your data.

I. What We Store

Everything stays on your device. Each formulation you log — its Maison, name, opened date, PAO, category, valuation, and ingredient text — is written to SwiftData, Apple's local persistence framework. No account is required to use the atelier. No password. No email. You are anonymous to us.

II. iCloud Synchronization (Optional)

If you enable Cabinet Sync, your formulations mirror across every Apple device signed into your iCloud account. This passes through Apple's CloudKit infrastructure under your Apple ID — Atelier Rohan never sees the data in transit. You can disable sync at any time from Settings → Cabinet Sync, returning the catalog to local-only custody. Changes to this preference take effect on next launch.

III. La Registre (Open Beauty Facts)

When you search for a product in the Add sheet, the query you type is sent to the Open Beauty Facts API — a public, community-built database of approximately 150,000 cosmetic products. No user identifier accompanies the request; the API receives only the search text and a generic User-Agent string. Open Beauty Facts is operated independently of Atelier Rohan. See openbeautyfacts.org for their terms.

IV. L'Analyseur (Optical Scanner)

When you use the ingredient scanner, your camera feed is processed entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework. No images are saved. No frames are transmitted. The recognized text is written to your local cabinet alongside the formulation you create.

V. Oxidation Advisories

Notifications are scheduled as local advisories by iOS, fired thirty days before each formulation crosses its Period After Opening expiry. Nothing is sent to a remote server. You can disable them at any time in Settings → Formula Correspondence.

VI. Analytics & Tracking

There are none. Atelier Rohan does not embed third-party analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or tracking pixels. The atelier does not know how many times you have opened the app or which screens you have visited.

VII. Data Deletion

To clear your cabinet, navigate to Settings → Inventory Storage → Delete All Formulations. This permanently removes every formulation from local storage and, if Cabinet Sync was enabled, from iCloud across all your devices. The deletion is immediate and irreversible. To delete a single formulation, long-press the card and tap Delete.

VIII. Children

Atelier Rohan is not designed for users under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect data from minors.

IX. Revisions to this Notice

Any material revision to this notice will be shipped as an update to the app and is dated at the top of this document. By continuing to use Atelier Rohan after a revision, you accept the updated notice.

X. Correspondence

For privacy questions, you may write to us by way of the Correspondence page.
