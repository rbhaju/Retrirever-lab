Correspondence.

We are a small studio, and we read every letter ourselves. Whether you have found an irregularity, wish to propose a refinement, or simply have a question about the care of your cabinet, you are most welcome to write to us. We reply, as a rule, within a few days — by hand, never by form letter.

Write to us at rohanbhaju@gmail.com.

When reporting an irregularity, kindly include your device and iOS version, that we might attend to it precisely.

Questions
Where is my cabinet kept?
Entirely on your device. The atelier holds no server, and we never see what you collect.
Is there a subscription?
There is not. A single payment of $9.99 grants the complete atelier, for the life of the application.
Will my cabinet follow me across devices?
If you keep iCloud, your formulations move privately between your own devices — directly, through Apple, never through us.
How does the optical scanner read a label?
It reads the period-after-opening marker upon the product itself, in full, upon your device. No photograph is ever sent elsewhere.
From where do the maison details come?
From Open Beauty Facts, an open registry. We query it only to autofill a brand, an ingredient list, or a barcode at your request.
Must I create an account?
Never. There is no account, no password, and no electronic mail required to begin.
May I export my collection?
Yes. Your records are yours; they may be drawn out at any moment and taken wherever you wish.
