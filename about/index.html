Hi, I'm Rohan
Founder · Retriever Labs · Sydney
By day I'm a systems engineer in Sydney, working on large, high-stakes tech environments where reliability and doing things properly really matter. That background taught me to care about the details most people never see, the kind that quietly decide whether something is trustworthy or not.
But what I genuinely love is designing and dreaming up apps that help people. Retriever Labs is where I get to do that on my own terms: calm, privacy-first tools that solve a real everyday problem without asking for your data in return.
Kepto is the first. It came from a simple frustration, that receipts fade and warranties lapse unnoticed, and the proof you need is never where you left it. I wanted something that quietly keeps that proof for you, entirely on your own phone. If that's the kind of software you like too, I'd love for you to try what I'm building.
