How it works
STEP 1
Connect
Link your box office or ticketing flow. We map your events, sessions and seat data — no replacement of your existing system.
STEP 2
Issue
Every buyer gets an Apple or Google Wallet pass the moment they purchase — branded with your venue's logo and colours.
STEP 3
Scan in
At the door, staff scan the pass and the guest is in. Works on the hardware you already have, even when the line is moving fast.
