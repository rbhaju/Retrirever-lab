Who we are
We build things
worth keeping.
Retriever Labs is a small, independent studio. We make calm, privacy-first apps that do one thing genuinely well, and respect the people who use them. No dark patterns, no harvesting your data, no clutter. Just useful tools, carefully made.
What we believe
Your data is yours
We build offline-first. Where we can, nothing leaves your device. Privacy isn't a feature. It's the default.
One thing, done well
We'd rather make a focused tool that's a joy to use than a bloated one that does everything badly.
Made with care
Details matter. We sweat the typography, the motion, the feel, because good craft is a form of respect.

Regulars
Turn coffee buyers into regulars
Live now
A digital stamp card that lives in your customers' Apple & Google Wallet. No app, no paper, and no work at the counter. Customers join and collect stamps with a quick QR scan, balances update automatically on their phone, and for the first time you see exactly who your regulars are.
