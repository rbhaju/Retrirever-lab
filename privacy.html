Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 June 2026

Kepto is built on one principle: your data belongs to you, and it stays with you. This policy explains, in plain language, what that means in practice.

The short version

  • Everything you add to Kepto — items, receipts, photos, prices, serial numbers — is stored only on your device.
  • We do not run a server that holds your data. We never see it.
  • We do not collect, sell, share, or monetise your personal information.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not use third-party trackers or analytics that identify you.
  • Backups and exports happen only when you choose to start them, and they go to your own iCloud or Google account — never to us.

If you remember nothing else: nothing leaves your phone unless you deliberately send it somewhere yourself.

What Kepto stores

When you use Kepto, you may add information such as:

  • Item names, categories, and the room they're in
  • Purchase dates, prices, and store names
  • Warranty length and expiry dates
  • Serial numbers
  • Photos of receipts and of the items themselves
  • Notes you write

All of this is saved locally in Kepto's private storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party as part of normal use.

What we do not collect

  • We do not create an account for you, and we do not require an email, phone number, or login.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, messages, or other apps' data.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not build a profile of you or your belongings.

Because we don't operate a server that receives your data, we have no copy of your information to lose, leak, sell, or hand over.

Camera and photos

Kepto uses your camera so you can scan receipts and photograph items. Images you capture are saved on your device. The text recognition that reads your receipt (OCR) runs on your device — the receipt image is not uploaded anywhere to be processed.

Kepto only accesses the camera and photo library when you actively use those features, and only with the permission your phone's operating system asks you to grant. You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings.

Backups and exports — only when you choose

Kepto offers two optional ways to move your data, and both are turned off until you start them:

Backup to your own cloud. If you enable it, Kepto can back up your data to your personal iCloud (Apple) or Google Drive (Android) account. This backup is tied to your account and protected by that provider's encryption. The data goes directly from your device to your cloud storage. We are not involved and cannot access it. This feature is off by default.

Export a report. When you generate an insurance or inventory report, Kepto creates a file (such as a PDF) on your device. What happens to that file next is entirely up to you — you decide whether to save it, email it, or share it. We never receive a copy.

When you use these features, your data becomes subject to the privacy policy of the service you send it to (for example, Apple's or Google's). We encourage you to review theirs.

Crash and performance data

If Kepto uses your operating system's standard crash-reporting (for example, Apple's or Google's built-in tools), those reports contain technical diagnostic information to help us fix bugs. They are handled by the platform provider under their privacy terms and are not used to identify you. Where your platform lets you opt out of sharing diagnostics, that choice is respected.

Children's privacy

Kepto is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because Kepto doesn't collect personal data on a server in the first place, no such data is gathered by us regardless of who uses the app.

Your control over your data

Because everything lives on your device, you are always in control:

  • Delete an item, and it's removed from your device's storage.
  • Delete the app, and all locally stored Kepto data is removed with it. (If you enabled cloud backup, remember to remove that copy from your iCloud or Google account separately.)
  • Revoke permissions (camera, photos) anytime in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Kepto handles data, we'll update this policy and revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will be highlighted within the app.
