Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your device. Kepto has no server and no account — nothing is uploaded unless you choose to export or back it up yourself. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I back up my items?
Backup is optional and goes to your own iCloud. You can enable it in Settings inside the app. It's off by default.

Will my receipts sync across devices?
Because Kepto keeps everything on-device for privacy, data lives on the phone where you added it. An optional backup to your own cloud lets you restore onto a new device.

I lost my phone — can you recover my data?
We can't, because we never have a copy. If you enabled iCloud backup, you can restore from your own account.
